Score every lead from 0–100:
- **Budget Potential (25 points)**: 25 = $2,000+ budget, 20 = $1k–$2k, 15 = moderate, 5 = low.
- **Need (20 points)**: 20 = obvious immediate developer need, 10 = possible need.
- **Skill Match (20 points)**: 20 = excellent match with React/Tailwind/HTML/CSS/Node.
- **Client Quality (15 points)**: 15 = legitimate founder/business/agency.
- **Project Quality (10 points)**: 10 = excellent portfolio/case-study opportunity.
- **Relationship Potential (10 points)**: 10 = recurring/long-term potential.
